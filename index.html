How we research

We review publicly available manufacturer information, published specifications, warranty details and independent sources where available. We distinguish manufacturer claims from editorial analysis and do not claim hands-on testing unless an article explicitly says so. Product availability, prices and program terms can change; always confirm details with the seller before purchasing.
