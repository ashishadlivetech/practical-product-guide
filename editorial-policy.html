OUR STANDARDS

Editorial policy

Research and sourcing

Articles should be based on identifiable sources such as manufacturer documentation, product manuals, public support pages, independent testing and clearly attributed customer feedback. Time-sensitive claims should include dates and be rechecked before publication.

Accuracy and corrections

We distinguish verified facts, manufacturer claims and editorial analysis. If we identify a material error, we aim to correct it and update the article date where appropriate. Readers can report corrections through our contact email.

Reviews and testing

We do not describe desk research as hands-on testing. Any future hands-on testing will explain the test method, duration and limitations.

Commercial relationships

Affiliate links and other material commercial relationships must be disclosed clearly. Compensation does not buy a positive conclusion.

Use of AI tools

AI tools may assist research organization, drafting and editing. A human editor remains responsible for checking sources, claims, disclosures and final publication. We do not publish unreviewed automated content or fabricated experiences.
